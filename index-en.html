About Us

Vantara

Vantara is a Peruvian agri-tech startup founded by Software Engineering students from the Peruvian University of Applied Sciences (UPC). It was created to address the livestock industry's main challenges through accessible digital tools that help manage livestock efficiently, traceably, and sustainably.

Livestock producers

Hatarium is an intuitive SaaS platform that lets you manage your livestock operations in real time. From anywhere, you can record animal health, feeding, reproductive cycles, and group assignments.

Veterinarians

Hatarium is a comprehensive clinical tool that makes it easier to manage veterinary work in the field and in the office. It centralizes medical records, streamlines diagnosis entry, and helps you keep structured track of treatments and appointments.

For Producers

Digital Livestock Records

Create individual health records and easily organize your animals into management groups.

Feeding and Reproductive Management

Assign feeding plans and monitor reproductive cycles (pregnancy, birth, dry-off, and weaning).

Automated Alerts

Get reminders about pending vaccinations and disease prevention.



For Veterinarians

Medical Records Access

Review your clients' complete livestock medical histories at any time.

Schedule and Appointment Management

Receive care requests and organize your field visits.

Diagnosis Records

Make field diagnoses, prescribe treatments, and record vaccinations.
